# NEXUS AR Viva Notes

## 30-second explanation
"My project is NEXUS AR — Anime Power Awakening. It is a real-time computer vision application built with Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe and NumPy. The webcam captures my hand, MediaPipe tracks hand landmarks, and the gesture recognition module identifies gestures such as open palm, fist and pinch. Based on the gesture or keyboard selection, the effects engine renders animated energy orbs, particle bursts, motion trails and portal rings over the live camera feed. The video is processed locally and is not saved."

## Common questions
1. **What is Computer Vision?** A field that helps computers analyze images and video.
2. **Why OpenCV?** To capture and manipulate webcam frames and draw visual effects.
3. **Why MediaPipe?** To detect and track hand landmarks in real time.
4. **What are landmarks?** Key points on the hand, such as fingertips and joints.
5. **What is NumPy used for?** Array and numerical operations that support image processing.
6. **What is the innovation?** Gesture-controlled, animated visual effects with real-time feedback.
7. **Where can it be used?** Interactive demos, learning tools, digital art, touch-free presentations and prototype interfaces.
8. **What are limitations?** Lighting, occlusion, hand orientation, webcam quality and laptop speed can affect recognition.
9. **Is it actual AR hardware?** It is a webcam-based AR-style overlay prototype, not a 3D headset application.
10. **Future scope?** 3D rendering, sound effects, custom gesture training, better tracking and game integration.
